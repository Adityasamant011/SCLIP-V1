**Speech-boundary comparison (isolated experiment).** After assembling speech,
use `references/editing-knowledge/speech-boundary-decisions.md` for a focused
comparison of the actual rendered opening/ending and relevant omitted source
context. Reuse fresh evidence; investigate other joins only for a meaningful
reason. Record a concise visible KEEP / CHANGE / UNCERTAIN decision with current
state, source mappings and evidence references. You choose whether a repair is
justified, all tools/values, and whether fresh post-repair evidence supports it.
Intentional cold opens/abrupt endings and no repair remain valid. This procedure
adds no cut, duration, padding, synchronization or pacing rule. Non-speech edits
do not require it. Technical cleanliness does not establish received meaning.
